Daughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford · 5th of 8 Siblings

Maude "Maudie" Ford

April 23, 1885 — 1946

Maude Ford married Timothy Tyler and her descendants form one of the most deeply rooted branches of the Ford family — running through the Tyler, Stewart, and Rouse families all the way to Chicago 2027.

Lineage Hiram & Jemima Ford→ Maude Ford→ Timita Tyler→ Marguerite Stewart→ Lem & Marguerite Rouse→ Chicago 2027

Maude Ford & Timothy Tyler

Maude "Maudie" Ford
April 23, 1885 — 1946
Daughter of James Hiram Ford & Jemima Anderson

Born in Michigan. One of eight children of Hiram & Jemima Ford. Her marriage to Timothy Tyler continued the Ford legacy into Wisconsin and Indiana, connecting the Ford family to the Stewart and Rouse lines.

&
TT
Timothy Tyler
1871 — 1920
Husband of Maude Ford

Born in Indiana in 1871. Timothy married Maude Ford and together they built a family that would carry the Ford name forward through their daughter Timita, born in Wisconsin in 1904.

Lemuel Hugh Rouse
Aug 17, 1913 — 1975
Son of Earl Rouse & Catherine Maude Lucas · South Bend, IN

Married Marguerite Stewart on August 31, 1934. Together they raised nine children at 1118 N. Burns Street, South Bend — a home full of Sunday dinners, cinnamon rolls, and memories.
