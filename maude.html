The Ford Family Line · Daughter of Hiram & Jemima

Maude "Maudie" Ford

April 23, 1885 — 1946

Maude Ford married Timothy Tyler and her descendants form one of the most deeply rooted branches of the Ford family — a line that runs through the Tyler, Stewart, and Rouse families all the way to Chicago 2027.

Lineage Hiram & Jemima Ford → Maude Ford → Timita Tyler → Marguerite Stewart → Lem & Marguerite Rouse → Chicago 2027

MF
Maude "Maudie" Ford
April 23, 1885 — 1946
Daughter of James Hiram Ford & Jemima Anderson

Born in Michigan. One of eight children of Hiram & Jemima Ford. Her marriage to Timothy Tyler continued the Ford legacy into Wisconsin and Indiana, connecting the Ford family to the Stewart and Rouse lines.

&
TT
Timothy Tyler
1871 — 1920
Husband of Maude Ford

Born in Indiana in 1871. Timothy married Maude Ford and together they built a family that would carry the Ford name forward through their daughter Timita, born in Wisconsin in 1904.

Timita Tyler & Samuel Stewart
TT
Timita Tyler
1904 — ? · Born Wisconsin
Daughter of Maude Ford & Timothy Tyler · Granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford

Timita was born in Wisconsin in 1904 — the home state connection to Hiram & Jemima Ford's Midwestern roots. She married Samuel Stewart and settled in Indiana, where they raised their family including their daughter Marguerite.

&
SS
Samuel Stewart
1897 — ? · Born Virginia
Husband of Timita Tyler

Born in Virginia in 1897. Son of George D. Stewart and Alice (1868–). Samuel's father George later married Lucy Fanlkner of Radford, Virginia. Samuel and Timita built their life in Indiana — the home state of Timita's grandfather James Hiram Ford.

Born in South Bend. Great-granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford. Married Lemuel Hugh Rouse on August 31, 1934. The heart of the Rouse family story.

Generation Three

Lemuel & Marguerite Rouse

1913–1975 & 1918–1967 · 1118 N. Burns Street, South Bend, Indiana

LR
Lemuel Hugh Rouse
Aug 17, 1913 — 1975
Son of Earl Rouse & Catherine Maude Lucas · Kokomo, Indiana

Lemuel was born August 17, 1913 in Kokomo, Indiana — a son of Earl Rouse and Catherine Maude Lucas. He married Marguerite Stewart on August 31, 1934 in St. Joseph County, Indiana. Together they raised their family at 1118 N. Burns Street, South Bend — a home full of Sunday dinners, cinnamon rolls, and memories that live on in this family.

MR
Marguerite Stewart Rouse
Oct 12, 1918 — Jan 1967
Great-granddaughter of James Hiram Ford & Jemima Anderson

Born in South Bend, Indiana. Daughter of Timita Tyler & Samuel Stewart. Granddaughter of Maude Ford. Great-granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford. Her funeral drew more people than the family had ever seen in one place — "the funeral of a rock star," as Kathy remembered it.
