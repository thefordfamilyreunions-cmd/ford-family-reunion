Marguerite
Stewart Rouse

October 12, 1918 — January 1967 · South Bend, Indiana

Great-granddaughter of James Hiram Ford and Jemima Anderson. Wife of Lemuel Hugh Rouse. Mother of nine. The heart of this entire family line.

"She had the funeral of a rock star. I had never seen so many people in one place at one time in all of my life."

— Kathy, remembering her mother Marguerite

Lemuel & Marguerite Rouse

LR
Lemuel Hugh Rouse
August 17, 1913 — 1975
Son of Earl Rouse & Catherine Maude Lucas · Kokomo, Indiana

Lemuel was born August 17, 1913 in Kokomo, Indiana. He married Marguerite Stewart on August 31, 1934 in St. Joseph County, Indiana. Together they built a family and a home at 1118 N. Burns Street, South Bend — a place of Sunday dinners, cinnamon rolls, and memories that live on in every person on this page.

&
MR
Marguerite Stewart Rouse
October 12, 1918 — January 1967
Great-granddaughter of James Hiram Ford & Jemima Anderson

Born in South Bend, Indiana. Daughter of Timita Tyler and Samuel Stewart. Granddaughter of Maude Ford. Great-granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima. Marguerite raised nine children and left a legacy so powerful that her passing drew a crowd none of them had ever seen. She is the heart of this line.

Mama made the best rolls in the entire state. We hated that the preacher came for Sunday dinner and always had to have the first piece of chicken — and the first roll to boot! Sundays were always like a feast at 1118. Food for royalty.

— Kathy, remembering 1118 N. Burns Street
