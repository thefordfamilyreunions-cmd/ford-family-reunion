Recipe from the Archive
Sunday Cinnamon Rolls
As made at 1118 N. Burns St. · South Bend, Indiana
Family Classic

"Mama made the best rolls in the entire state. We hated that the preacher always got the first one — and the biggest piece of chicken to boot." — Kathy Rouse, from the 2013 Reunion program

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • ¼ cup sugar + extra for filling
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cinnamon
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • Cream cheese glaze to finish

Method

  • Dissolve yeast in warm milk with a pinch of sugar. Let sit 5 min until foamy.
  • Mix flour, sugar, salt. Add yeast mixture, egg, and butter. Knead 8 minutes until smooth.
  • Cover and let rise 1 hour until doubled.
  • Roll out on a floured surface into a rectangle. Spread with butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.
  • Roll tightly, slice into 12 pieces, place in buttered pan.
  • Rise 30 more minutes, then bake at 375°F for 20-22 minutes.
  • Glaze while warm. Share with the family — the preacher gets in line like everyone else.
