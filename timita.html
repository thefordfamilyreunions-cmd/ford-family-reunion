Timita Tyler & Samuel Stewart

TT
Timita Tyler
1904 — ? · Born Wisconsin
Daughter of Maude Ford & Timothy Tyler · Granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford

Born in Wisconsin in 1904, Timita is the granddaughter of James Hiram Ford and Jemima Anderson. She married Samuel Stewart and settled in Indiana — the home state of her grandfather James Hiram Ford. Together they raised three children, including Marguerite, whose marriage to Lemuel Rouse would produce one of the most storied family lines in the Ford legacy.

&
SS
Samuel Stewart
1897 — ? · Born Virginia
Husband of Timita Tyler

Born in Virginia in 1897, son of George D. Stewart and Alice. Samuel and Timita built their life together in Indiana, where they raised Alice, Marguerite, and George Frederick. Samuel's family roots in Virginia add another thread to the rich tapestry of the Ford family story.
