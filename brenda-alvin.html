Marc Drake
Son of Brenda & Alvin · Great-great-great-grandson of Hiram & Jemima Ford
📍 Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Marc is an Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) physician practicing in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. An accomplished doctor who represents the talent and drive that runs deep in the Ford family. When he's not in the clinic, you'll find him on two wheels — cycling is his passion, and Biscotti the dog is usually nearby.

Sydney Gates
Daughter of Brenda & Alvin · Great-great-great-granddaughter of Hiram & Jemima Ford
