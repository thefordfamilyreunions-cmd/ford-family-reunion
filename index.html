The Vision

We've been celebrating branches.
It's time to celebrate the tree.

The Ford Family Legacy site is home to ALL descendants of James Hiram Ford and Jemima Anderson — the first place that brings together ALL descendants of James Hiram Ford and Jemima Anderson — Jasper's family, Maude's family, and every sibling's line. Many of us have never met. That changes in Chicago, July 2027.
