Every name has a face. Every face has a story. This is our family — from the founders who started it all, to the branches spreading across the country today.

These are the people behind the names in our family tree. Photos will grow over time — if you have a photo to share, we want it here. Every face added is a piece of history preserved.

The Root

James Hiram Ford & Jemima Anderson

Founder
JHF
James Hiram Ford
1849 — 1919 · Indiana & Cassapolis, MI

Son of William Ford & Priscilla Casey. Married Jemima Anderson in 1867. Father of eight children. Died August 5, 1919 in Cassapolis, Michigan.

Founder
JA
Jemima Anderson Ford
1841 — 1916 · Michigan & Cassapolis, MI

Daughter of Jefferson Anderson & Jalitha Madrey-Anderson. Born in Michigan. Married James Hiram Ford. Died August 3, 1916 in Cassapolis, Michigan.

Lemuel & Marguerite Rouse

Elder
LR
Lemuel Hugh Rouse
Aug 17, 1913 — 1975

Son of Earl Rouse & Catherine Maude Lucas. Married Marguerite Stewart on August 31, 1934. Father of nine children raised at 1118 N. Burns Street, South Bend.
